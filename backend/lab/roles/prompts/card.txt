Build a source-grounded card of the CUSTOMER of one recorded support chat. A simulator will play this customer against a new version of the support agent described in `agent` (its domain and the identifiers its customers give), so keep the customer's task, knowledge and manner, never the old agent's answers.
The chat is the customer's whole conversation with the bank assistant; several agents may have answered. Find the episode: the CUSTOMER event n where the customer first states their task of the agent's domain (episode.start) and how the customer got there (entry: first_message | after_greeting | after_other_topic). When episodeStart is given, the episode starts at that event: use it as episode.start. When episodeEnd is given, the episode ends at that event: what comes after it is another task, so cite nothing after it.
Evidence: every customer-side item cites a CUSTOMER event n and an exact quote copied from it (3-150 characters). Agent context cites an AGENT event agentN and an exact quote from it.
- Only what the customer actually wrote. No biography, role, age, emotions or reasons the text does not show.
- circumstances: the customer's situation in the world (equipment, point of sale, what already happened), never remarks about the chat itself.
- Absence is not knowledge. If the chat never shows whether the customer knows something, list it in notEstablished (identifiers have their own field). does_not_know needs the customer's own words.
- A value the customer typed but the export masked (# for digits, * for hidden text) is masked_in_source: the customer knew it.
- What the AGENT said is never the customer's initial knowledge: such facts are learned_from_agent.
- said: opening (in the episode's first message) | later (volunteered later) | on_request (answering the agent's question).
- observations: the customer's own attempts in the world (pressed a button on the device, opened a section, followed a link) and what happened, in their words. Never an agent answer, never the customer's reply.
- A result the customer got by following the agent's suggestion is an observation, never a fact: the new agent may suggest something else.
- reactions: how the customer answered a specific agent move, one for each customer message that answers an agent message. Observed ones only, each with the agent quote it answered and the customer quote. trigger names what that agent message did:
  unclear_question: asked a question the customer did not understand or could not answer; repeated_clarification: asked again what was already asked; wrong_object: answered about another object or task than the customer's; inapplicable_instruction: gave steps the customer cannot or need not follow; no_progress: replied without moving the task forward; identifier_request: asked for one of agent.identifiers or other account details; choice_offer: asked to choose between options; instruction: gave steps to follow; handoff_offer: offered an operator, a specialist, support or a phone line; resolved: answered the question.
- identifiers: for each of agent.identifiers, by its key: {status:"knows|masked_in_source|does_not_know|not_established",n,quote}; n and quote only for the first three. knows and masked_in_source need a quote with the value itself (its digits or its mask); the name of a company, shop or person is not an identifier: keep it as a fact.
- Never put the correct agent answer, a bank procedure or a quality criterion into customer fields.
- openingFilled: the episode's first customer message character for character, each masked run (# or *) replaced by one plausible fictional value of its kind (digits for numbers); the same text if nothing is masked.
domainDoubt: empty, or a few words why the customer seems to have no task of the agent's domain at all (one of agent.excludes); the card is still built.
Use Russian; write text, goal, object, response and action/result addressed to the customer in the second person («у тебя», «ты»). Return {domainDoubt, name:"business title, 3-7 words", episode:{start, entry}, goal, object, circumstances:[{text,n,quote}], facts:[{text,status:"knows|believes|learned_from_agent|does_not_know|masked_in_source",said,n,quote}], notEstablished:[text], observations:[{action,result,n,quote}], reactions:[{trigger,response,agentN,agentQuote,n,quote}], identifiers:{<key>:{status,n,quote}}, openingFilled}
