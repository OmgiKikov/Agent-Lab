Build a source-grounded card of the CUSTOMER of one recorded support chat. A simulator will play this customer against a new version of the bank's acquiring support agent, so keep the customer's task, knowledge and manner, never the old agent's answers.
The chat is the customer's whole conversation with the bank assistant; several agents may have answered. Find the acquiring episode: the CUSTOMER event n where the acquiring task is first stated (episode.start) and how the customer got there (entry: first_message | after_greeting | after_other_topic). When episodeStart is given, the episode starts at that event: use it as episode.start.
Evidence: every customer-side item cites a CUSTOMER event n and an exact quote copied from it (3-150 characters). Agent context cites an AGENT event agentN and an exact quote from it.
- Only what the customer actually wrote. No biography, role, age, emotions or reasons the text does not show.
- circumstances: the customer's situation in the world (equipment, point of sale, what already happened), never remarks about the chat itself.
- Absence is not knowledge. If the chat never shows whether the customer knows something, list it in notEstablished (identifiers have their own field). does_not_know needs the customer's own words.
- A value the customer typed but the export masked (# for digits, * for hidden text) is masked_in_source: the customer knew it.
- What the AGENT said is never the customer's initial knowledge: such facts are learned_from_agent.
- said: opening (in the episode's first message) | later (volunteered later) | on_request (answering the agent's question).
- observations: the customer's own attempts in the world (pressed a button on the device, opened a section, followed a link) and what happened, in their words. Never an agent answer, never the customer's reply.
- A result the customer got by following the agent's suggestion is an observation, never a fact: the new agent may suggest something else.
- reactions: how the customer answered a specific agent move, one for each customer message that answers an agent message. Observed ones only, each with the agent quote it answered and the customer quote. trigger names what that agent message did:
  unclear_question: asked a question the customer did not understand or could not answer; repeated_clarification: asked again what was already asked; wrong_object: answered about another object or task than the customer's; inapplicable_instruction: gave steps the customer cannot or need not follow; no_progress: replied without moving the task forward; identifier_request: asked for a terminal number, INN, merchant or contract; choice_offer: asked to choose between options; instruction: gave steps to follow; handoff_offer: offered an operator, a specialist, support or a phone line; resolved: answered the question.
- hypotheses: at most two plausible reactions to triggers that did NOT occur in this chat, tentative and consistent with the observed manner; they are untested. They describe only how the customer answers (clarifies, repeats, asks for a human, says they will try), never new facts, numbers, past behaviour («как раньше», «обычно») or results of actions the customer did not report, and never contradict an observed reaction.
- identifiers.terminal (the terminal number) and identifiers.organization (the INN or bank details of the company): {status:"knows|masked_in_source|does_not_know|not_established",n,quote}; n and quote only for the first three. knows and masked_in_source need a quote with the value itself (its digits or its mask); a merchant, shop or company name is not the INN or a terminal number: keep it as a fact.
- Never put the correct agent answer, a bank procedure or a quality criterion into customer fields.
- openingFilled: the episode's first customer message character for character, each masked run (# or *) replaced by one plausible fictional value of its kind (digits for numbers); the same text if nothing is masked.
eligible is false when the customer has no acquiring task; ineligibleReason says why. Not acquiring: greeting only, paying as a buyer, salary projects, other bank products. When in doubt, eligible is true: the chat reached the acquiring agent.
