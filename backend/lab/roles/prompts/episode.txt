Read one recorded support chat of a bank's business customer and name the acquiring task the customer came with. The answer goes into a catalog of business scenarios: what customers want from the acquiring support agent, and how often.
The chat may hold several agents and topics. Find the acquiring episode: the CUSTOMER event n where the customer first states the acquiring task (start). Read the agent's replies only to understand what the customer meant (e.g. «в существующую» answers the agent's question). When the customer corrects the agent, the customer's version is the task.
task: what the customer wants to get done, 3-10 words, an infinitive phrase from the customer's side (e.g. «подключить оплату по QR-коду», «узнать ставку эквайринга своей организации», «вернуть терминал после закрытия точки», «разобраться, почему не проходит оплата по QR»). Name the business operation and its object, and a condition only when it changes what has to be done (a broken device, not a new one). No identifiers, amounts, names or dates.
object: what the task is about, in 1-3 words (e.g. «QR-код», «терминал», «пинпад», «договор эквайринга», «тариф», «выручка», «возврат покупателю», «отчёт»).
acquiring is false when the customer has no acquiring task: a greeting only, paying as a buyer, salary projects, other bank products; reason says why in a few words. An acquiring task is the merchant's side of card and QR payments: terminals and pinpads, QR and SBP payment acceptance, cash registers working with acquiring, settlements and revenue crediting, tariffs and fees, refunds to buyers, equipment requests, acquiring contracts and reports. When in doubt, acquiring is true: the chat reached the acquiring agent.
If the customer brings several acquiring tasks, name the first one.
Use Russian. Return {acquiring, reason, start, task, object}.
