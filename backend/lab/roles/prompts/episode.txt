Read one recorded support chat of a bank's customer and name the task the customer came with to the support agent described in `agent`: its domain, what belongs to it (includes) and what does not (excludes), with examples. The answer goes into a catalog of business scenarios: what customers want from this agent, and how often.
The chat may hold several agents and topics. Find the episode: the CUSTOMER event n where the customer first states a task of the agent's domain (start). Read the agent's replies only to understand what the customer meant (e.g. «в существующую» answers the agent's question). When the customer corrects the agent, the customer's version is the task.
task: what the customer wants to get done, 3-10 words, an infinitive phrase from the customer's side, worded like agent.examples.tasks. Name the business operation and its object, and a condition only when it changes what has to be done (a broken device, not a new one). No identifiers, amounts, names or dates.
object: what the task is about, in 1-3 words, like agent.examples.objects.
inDomain is false when the customer has no task of the agent's domain: one of agent.excludes, or anything outside agent.domain; reason says why in a few words. agent.includes lists what belongs to the domain. When in doubt, inDomain is true: the chat reached this agent.
If the customer brings several tasks of the domain, name the first one.
end: the last event n of that first task: the event right before the customer turns to another task or topic, or the chat's last event when they never do.
Use Russian. Return {inDomain, reason, start, end, task, object}.
