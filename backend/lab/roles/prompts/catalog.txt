Build a catalog of business scenarios of a bank's acquiring support from the tasks real customers came with. Each input item is one distinct task: the task, its object and count, how many customer episodes have it (frequent tasks matter more).
Group the tasks into categories and, inside each category, scenarios.
- A category is the business area of the request: a product or an object of acquiring (e.g. «QR-оплата и СБП», «Терминалы и пинпады», «Тарифы и комиссии», «Возвраты покупателям», «Зачисление выручки», «Документы и отчёты», «Подключение и отключение эквайринга»).
- A scenario is one customer task inside a category, named as the customer would name it (e.g. in «QR-оплата и СБП»: «Не работает оплата по QR», «Подключить оплату по QR», «Отключить QR»). Split two scenarios only when the customer's goal, the object, or a condition that changes what has to be done differs; another wording of the same task is the same scenario.
- Every input task must fit one scenario. No vague catch-all like «Прочее» or «Другие вопросы» for a large group: name what those customers want. A scenario covers at least two tasks unless a task is clearly unlike any other.
- Group by the customer's goal, never by the agent's answer, the channel or a quality problem (a wrong answer, a transfer to an operator).
description: for a category, one sentence on what it covers; for a scenario, one or two sentences on what customers want and how to tell it from the neighbouring scenarios. Only the customer's side: never the correct agent answer, a bank procedure or a quality criterion.
At most 12 categories, at most 8 scenarios in a category.
Use Russian. Return {categories:[{title, description, scenarios:[{title, description}]}]}.
