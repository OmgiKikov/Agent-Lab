<objective>
Make the run's number honest in four verified places (defects HN-1..HN-4 from the product review of 2026-09-24):

- HN-1 fail-first across attempts: a verified fail in a usable attempt decides the card even when another attempt is unusable or missing (src/run.ts).
- HN-2 tool channel: on a complete tool log a judged fail stands without a cited tool_result; a pass still needs one (src/card/expectations.ts).
- HN-3 headline: more than 10% not measured never shows «good», and the headline says how many are not measured (src/result-text.ts).
- HN-4 causes: Lab-side refusals, grading refusals and an adapter's reported measurementError get a non-agent cause instead of «агент не ответил» (src/evaluation.ts, src/lab/review.ts, contracts, wording).

Owner decisions that bind this plan:
- OD-1: agent silence (empty reply, timeout, 5xx) STAYS «не измерено» with the agent cause; it is never a fail.
- OD-2: fail-first: a usable attempt with a verified fail decides the card as failed; only a pass needs every attempt usable and matching.
- OD-3: the not-measured threshold is «more than 10% of counted situations»; the CI exit code (already 2 for anything unmeasured) is left as it is.

Purpose: the owner and the customer must be able to trust the number: a real failure is never hidden behind a later silent attempt, a proven missing action is never «не измерено», a thin measurement never looks green, and a Lab or connection problem is never blamed on the agent.
Output: the four fixes with tests; frozen fixtures still parse and derive.
</objective>
